# Write down why, not only what

## Summary

- Record the reason for a choice, not just the choice. ^summary

## Statement

Capture the reasoning behind a decision where others will find it.

## Why

The what is visible in the result. The why is lost unless someone writes it down.

## How it applies

Add the reason, the options you rejected and what would change your mind.

## Implications

Later readers can tell a good constraint from an old accident.
