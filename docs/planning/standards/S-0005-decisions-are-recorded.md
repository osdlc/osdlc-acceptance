# Decisions are recorded where the team can find them

## Summary

- Write each significant decision down in a shared, findable place. ^summary

## Rule

Record each significant decision, with its reason, in a place the whole team can find.

## Why

Unrecorded decisions get re-argued and forgotten.

## How to apply

Write a short note: the question, the choice, the reason and the options you rejected.
